> **note:** to my parents, or any of my close relatives/persons, please contact me in order to get the full details, as most of the details here are truncated for everyone's privacy and safety reasons, as i don't intend to harm the people that harass me, they also deserve some privacy.

hello there,

yeah, look at my previous post from literally six days ago: *"new social media, hello instagram"*. 

the comedic timing could not be more ironic if i tried. lol. well, that aged like milk.

if you read that post, you know i was trying to figure out a way to exist on instagram without completely wrecking my screen time and mental health. 

but honestly? even if i uninstalled the app and had zero communications on it, i still just feel fundamentally uncomfortable leaving my account to be managed by someone else — even if that person is a close family member or a close friend. at the end of the day, your personal identity is yours, and trying to separate yourself from it just never sits right.

and well, the whole thing was just a bad idea from the start.

---


i'm not going to get into all the specifics, names, or drama here — for privacy reasons, and because it really isn't worth giving it any more energy.

essentially, some stuff happened involving someone at school, and private information ended up in my DMs. looking back, it should have just been kept a secret and left alone. but because of my autism, i have this terrible habit of doing things impulsively and sharing or talking about things without thinking them through, or realizing what the consequences will actually be. it doesn't even stick in my memory, and before i even realize what happened, it's already out there. (i'm not even kidding.)

and once that happened, things blew completely out of proportion. (it was not fun.)

it triggered a massive wave of retaliation. what followed was honestly terrifying: endless threatening messages, targeted harassment from people at school, and non-stop login attempts, OTP spam, and people actively trying to hack into my accounts. all of this chaos eventually led to me almost getting a panic attack. (it was a whole mess.)

social media is basically a social minefield. for someone like me who already struggles with reading social situations and has terrible impulse control, it's literally just asking for disaster. trying to distance myself or have someone else in the loop didn't solve anything either — it just pulled more people into the crossfire and caused unnecessary stress for everyone involved. (lesson learned.)

but honestly? that wasn't even the biggest takeaway. the real realization was that social media — especially during school — is just completely toxic and not worth a single second of my sanity.

---


between the harassment, the threats, the constant anxiety, and everything else going on in life right now, it took an extreme toll on me. 

my marks have been dropping, my academics are suffering, and my health — both mental and physical — has taken a serious hit. it got to the point where all of this was damaging my health even more, to the point where i just felt: *i have to stop. i have to, I HAVE TO STOP.* 

ok. so i stopped.

i instantly went into every platform i had and deactivated all my social media accounts — instagram, linkedin, and whatever else i had lying around.

honestly, i was never truly comfortable on meta platforms anyway. between their data privacy shenanigans and the fact that instagram is flooded with unethical, poorly maintained brain rot... i am telling you right now: if you are under 16, quit instagram as soon as possible if you want to stay okay. (it's a cesspool of shit, go outside.)

---

i've decided to reduce my communications to almost a halt, at least on non-official stuff, and heavily cut down my screen time. here's what's actually happening:

- **meta (instagram and threads):** completely deactivated and uninstalled. i want nothing to do with it. (whatsapp is also meta, but it's utility messaging and at least less crap-ware than instagram or facebook. though honestly, what happened to the good old days of skype? those days are long gone.)
- **x (twitter):** heavily unmaintained. there's a very high chance i'll end up leaving X as well. for now it'll mostly just be automatic redirects for new blog posts, or occasionally replying to random posts i find funny (or unfunny).
- **pinterest & wattpad:** since i've been getting back into photography and literature/writing, i might occasionally use pinterest for photos or wattpad for writing, but i'm still very unsure.
- **youtube:** the only one left where i'm still somewhat active. you might see me commenting on videos, and i might post some screen recordings of testing software or voiceovers of blog posts (strictly without my face).
- **discord:** this is actually the official way to reach me alongside email ([debarkak@murena.io](mailto:debarkak@murena.io)). but heads up: i treat discord like emails now. i might take days to reply, or i might not reply at all. sorry if i can't reply back quickly, i'm just trying to keep my sanity.
- **this website:** this is basically where i dump everything anyway. if i make something, break something, or write a blog post, it'll end up right here. and if you want updates without dealing with any feeds, there's always the [rss feed](/rss.xml).

---

honestly, the whole concept of social media is just stupid now. everyone uses instagram to text their friends when that's not even what the app was made for. (what's whatsapp for then? where's skype??) 

and aside from the toxicity and drama, social media is also just disgusting when it comes to privacy and data collection. 

everything has basically turned into mass surveillance machines. they track every single click, how long you linger on a post, your location, your contacts, and your personal data. they build profiles on you, sell your info to advertisers, and scrape your posts to train AI models without you ever consenting. you're literally just the product for them to profit off of. giving up my private data and mental health to meta and other corporations just to get harassed by other humans makes zero sense. (seriously.)

i'm almost completely detached from social media at this point, and i doubt i'll ever use it again unless it's somehow absolutely necessary (which honestly doesn't even make sense, like why would it be necessary?).

right now, i just want to go outside, get some fresh air, probably do some exercises, be healthy, read books, focus on my studies, and do things that ARE NOT software development. because to be completely honest, i am starting to get bored. really bored. (i just don't want to turn into one of those energy-drink-guzzling nerds who only knows how to code.)

(and this doesn't mean i'm quitting my current dev projects. if anything, without all the sociality bullshit wasting hours of my day, i might actually have more time to work on things at a normal pace when i actually want to.) 

anyways, i will still be around ig, for now, goodbye.
