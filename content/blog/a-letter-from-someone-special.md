**My Dearest,**

For I write with my quill,
Your absence gives me heartache still,
Your presence—like the moonlight,
Guiding me through the darkest night.

Longing for true love has made me regret,
That why did life take so much time to get
Us together—I wonder,
If it is truly us, forever....

**Yours lovingly,**
Zara
